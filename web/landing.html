03 / BEFORE YOU START

A little context.
A clearer first run.

What to bring, what gets saved, and how to interpret the result.

What do I need to try it?

A public HTTPS endpoint you control or are authorized to call, a stable delivery reference and a JSON payload. Creating a job does not send it; Send delivery is a separate action.

How does receipt lookup work?

Provide a read-only HTTPS GET URL and JSON pointers that select the receipt array and reference field. Checkpoint accepts exactly one matching receipt. An empty or ambiguous result does not authorize another POST.

Does this guarantee exactly-once delivery?

No. A 2xx response is an HTTP acknowledgement, not independent proof of a business action. Retry safety depends on the destination’s documented idempotency contract or your explicit acknowledgement of duplicate risk.

Where do my data and credentials go?

Jobs and attempt journals are stored in your private server-side workspace. Your browser holds its access key. An optional authorization header is used only for the action you initiate and is not saved in job history or exports.
